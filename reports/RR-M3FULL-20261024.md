## Attribution: capture-time concurrency

The divergence is between the **capture baseline** (raw-v2, 2026-10-23) and the
(repeatedly reproduced) replay path. Evidence:

- `corpus-v2.capture.meta.json` is stamped by the *resume* pass (started ==
  finished, wall 0.0 s, `--resume` found all 480 done) ⇒ the real capture session
  ended **before 14:31:20Z**; the Hermes-side decide ledger was still alive until
  14:50:20Z. The xCABI ledgers carry no per-event timestamps, so the exact
  interleave cannot be proven, but file-level overlap of the two sessions is the
  plausible window.
- The suspect band's capture rows are latency-elevated (7/10 ≥ 17 s vs. global
  median 13.2 s; ex-308 21.0 s, ex-342 23.1 s, ex-345 26.6 s, ex-346 22.8 s,
  ex-232 19.2 s), while *other* slow episodes (ex-075–ex-150 ~24–30 s, ex-388
  33.5 s) produced **no** distribution divergence — slowness alone does not break
  the hash, only concurrent co-traffic does. RR-M3's v1 `ex-001` observation had
  exactly this signature (same pattern, same resolution).
- Both audits ran quiescently w.r.t. any observed client, and reproduce each other
  bit-for-bit.

**Reading:** the 10 rows are capture-side contamination — raw distributions
sampled while a second decide client shared the llama-server. The determinism
claim (v0 §3.3, quiescent same-build same-machine ⇒ byte-identical response) is
**not** violated by the replay path: cross-sample byte-identity held everywhere;
the capture rows were made under conditions outside the claim (RR-M3 caveat,
now at full-corpus scale). Decision keys/refs/identity were never affected.

## Caveats

- No per-event timestamps in xCABI ledgers ⇒ capture/concurrency overlap is
  inferred (mtimes, latency profile, resume meta), not proven per-decide.
- Replay reproducibility is same-build/same-machine (two server instances, one
  runner build); cross-machine/cross-build byte-stability is not claimed (v0 ω).
- Single corpus/pin/seed/machine; T13 release-gate wording rides in the manifest
  verbatim.
- `contentHash` is a hash over the exact serialized raw distribution — the audit
  intentionally treats last-digit probability drift as a mismatch (strictness by
  design, §3.3); substance is judged separately in this report.
