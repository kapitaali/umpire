## 1. Result

On the untouched 40-example test split, accuracy is **0.800 (32/40)** and
isotonic calibration cuts ECE from **0.1428** (raw entropy-complement) / **0.1747**
(raw top `p(key)`) to **0.0457**; Brier goes **0.1522 / 0.1699 → 0.1361**. The
selected method is **`isotonic/1`** (validation ECE 0.1094 vs 0.1547 for temperature
scaling), artifact `data_version` **`cal-v1:caf6a04f9e94b9a2`**. The paired-bootstrap
95% intervals on the test deltas include zero at this n=40, so the direction is
encouraging but **not decisive**; see §5.

The calibrated view is fitted **P(key is correct)** for the four-label, fixed-seed,
pinned-1.7B distribution. Raw probabilities and raw entropy-complement confidence are
untouched, and the decision identity (`ref`/`contentHash`) is unchanged by calibration.

## 2. Corpus (corpus-v1)

- 160 examples, **40 per label** (`billing`, `other`, `sales`, `technical`),
  project-authored and labeled under a written policy; not model-derived.
- Deterministic stratified split: **80 train / 40 validation / 40 test** (20/10/10 per
  label), seed `20261023`.
- Every state is a **caller-owned few-shot routing guide plus the customer message**
  (`state.text = ROUTING_GUIDE + message`); the original message is kept in the corpus
  row as `message` for audit. The guide is part of the calibrated input distribution,
  not hidden harness behavior.
- Provenance and the full label policy: `calibration/README.md`,
  `calibration/corpus-v1.meta.json`.

### Why a routing guide is in the state

A **56-example bare-text pilot** (preserved outside the repo at
`/tmp/opencode/m2-zeroshot-pilot/`, not a committed gate) reached **46.4%** accuracy.
The 1.7B collapsed `sales` into `billing`/`other` (14 of 15 sales examples wrong), and
several billing/technical cases were misrouted. Bare label uids were insufficient. A
fixed 12-example guide (three per label) raised a 10-case probe to 9/10, so the corpus
uses that guide in every state. The pilot is design evidence, not calibration data.

### Labeling policy (summary)

Gold is the primary action the sender needs; an explicit ask beats mentioned context.
`billing` = money/account administration on an existing subscription; `sales` =
pre-purchase evaluation or new revenue; `technical` = product malfunction/configuration/
usage; `other` = non-product inbound (press, careers, legal/privacy/security, research,
community, brand, sponsorship, vendor proposals). A refund request caused by a bug is
`billing`; a request to fix the bug is `technical`. Existing-account plan changes are
`billing`; new purchases are `sales`. Examples deliberately include cross-domain
distractors.

## 4. Calibration method

Two candidates were fitted on **train only** and compared on **validation** by ECE
(Brier tie-break); the winner was refit on **train+validation** and evaluated once on
**test**:

| method id | fit | calibrated confidence |
|---|---|---|
| `temperature-scaling/1` | temperature `T` minimizes gold-label NLL; `q_i ∝ p_i ** (1/T)` | `q[key]` |
| `isotonic/1` | pool-adjacent-violators from raw `p[key]` to correctness | `PAV(p[key])` |

Both candidates were fit on the 80 train examples; validation (40 examples) gave:

| method | fitted parameter | val ECE | val Brier |
|---|---|---|---|
| `temperature-scaling/1` | `T = 4.765917670659464` | 0.1547 | 0.1791 |
| `isotonic/1` | 80 PAV points | **0.1094** | 0.1918 |

The ECE-first rule selected **isotonic** despite its worse validation Brier; the
rule uses Brier only as an exact-ECE tie-break. The winner was refit on
train+validation (120 examples), producing 120 PAV points, and the test split was
evaluated once. `data_version` is `cal-v1:caf6a04f9e94b9a2`; it binds the corpus/raw
hashes, labels, seed, pin identity, method, statistic, and the 120 fitted points.
The artifact's in-sample calibrated ECE is 0 by construction (PAV fits the fit
split) and is not used as evidence.

Both candidates target `P(key is correct)`. Raw entropy-complement confidence and raw
top probability (`p[key]`) are reported as baselines so the calibration delta is
visible. The artifact keeps both candidates' validation scores, the selected
parameters, test metrics, reliability bins, and paired-bootstrap intervals.

`data_version` is a stable `cal-v1:<16 hex>` over the artifact core (corpus/raw hashes,
labels, seed, pin identity, method, statistic, fitted parameters). It is referenced by
every calibrated record; timestamps, host, and metrics are outside the core.

## 6. Integration and schema

- `spec/decision-record-v0.schema.json` keeps the same field set: the existing
  optional `calibrated` object (`method`, `data_version`, `confidence`) is used as
  designed. Only a description was added; no new fields and no required fields.
- `harness/umpire.py` accepts `calibration=<artifact>` (CLI `--calibration`), validates
  the artifact's self-hash, pin/seed/label identity **before** any model call, and
  appends the calibrated sidecar after the raw record is built. Raw response/hash/ref
  are unchanged.
- `spec/calibration_tools.py` is the shared math (temperature scaling, PAV, ECE/Brier,
  reliability, bootstrap, artifact core/version) used by the fitter, harness, and
  validator.
- `spec/validate_ledger.py` loads only structurally valid committed
  `calibration/umpire-cal-*.json` artifacts, recomputes calibrated confidence from raw
  probabilities, and rejects a record whose method, `data_version`, labels, `model_pin`,
  or seed does not match, or whose value does not recompute. A tampered/inconsistent
  artifact cannot justify a calibrated record. Two calibrated negatives were added to
  the built-in corpus (confidence mismatch, unknown version).
- `calibration/corpus.calibrated.records.jsonl` is the same 160-record sidecar with the
  calibrated block on the **test rows only**; it pairs with the same ledger (raw
  contentHash unchanged). `checksums.sha256` covers all nine data artifacts. Artifact
  provenance: fit at `git_head=02dce45`, `git_dirty_tracked=false`; capture metadata is
  bound by `capture_meta_sha256` and was produced from the pre-commit tree
  (`06accbd`, dirty).

## 8. Limitations and honesty

- The corpus is **project-authored** (160 synthetic support intents), not a production
  sample. Accuracy is a property of this corpus and the few-shot guide, not a general
  intent-router benchmark.
- The guide is caller-owned input; semantic overlap between guide examples and corpus
  examples is expected in few-shot prompting. It is not trained/fitted from the test
  labels.
- Calibration is **per pin, per label set, per seed, per machine/build**. Do not apply
  it to other labels, pins, seeds, or builds; the harness enforces pin/seed/label
  identity, and cross-hardware byte-stability is not claimed.
- The artifact is fitted to the corpus input distribution (`ROUTING_GUIDE + message`).
  The harness cannot verify the state text; applying it to a bare-text state is out of
  distribution and not covered by these metrics.
- The test split has **40 examples**; the reported ECE/Brier improvements are point
  estimates, and every paired-bootstrap 95% interval in §5 includes zero. Treat the
  delta as directional at this n, not as a decisive population claim. The in-sample
  calibrated ECE is 0 by construction and is not evidence.
- Calibration cannot fix semantic errors. One test error (gold `other` → `billing`)
  remains at calibrated confidence 1.0 because its raw top probability is 1.0; the
  calibrated [0.9,1.0] bin is 15/16 correct at mean confidence 0.973.
- The calibrated confidence is an estimate of P(key correct) on this distribution, not
  a correctness guarantee.
- Capture latency (median 13.1 s, max 30.4 s) is a local 4-core CPU measurement under
  load; it says nothing about hosted latency or the proposal's JEV band.
- The pilot is outside the repo and is design evidence only; it is not part of the
  committed corpus or artifact.
- ODPS packaging and the hub leaderboard are deferred; this report does not claim the
  full proposal M2 definition.
