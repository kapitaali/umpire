# RR-M2V2-20261023 — calibration corpus-v2 expansion

**Status:** complete 2026-10-23.  Local-only (T14); zero provider spend.
**Artifact:** `calibration/umpire-cal-v2.json`, `data_version`
`cal-v1:aceb881587b3b3a6`, method `isotonic/1`.
**Scope:** one pin (`qwen3-1.7b`), one fixed label set, seed 7, one machine/build.

## Result in one line

On the v2 **120-example untouched test split**, isotonic calibration reduces ECE
from **0.2483** (raw top probability) / **0.2110** (raw entropy-complement) to
**0.0267**, and Brier from **0.2380** to **0.1723**.  Accuracy is unchanged at
**0.7083**.  The paired-bootstrap 95% intervals exclude zero for both ECE and
Brier deltas vs raw top probability (`[-0.262469, -0.071803]` and
`[-0.114074, -0.017392]`).

This is a stronger local result than v1, not a production claim.  It remains one
project-authored corpus, one pin/seed/machine, and one untouched test sample.
The v1 n=40 result is still historical and directional; v2 does not retroactively
upgrade it.

## What changed

v1 was 160 examples (40/label, 80/40/40 train/validation/test).  Corpus-v2 is
**480 examples** (120/label) with a **240/120/120** split and a **120-example
test set**.  The v1 rows are preserved byte-for-byte and keep their original
splits; 320 new rows were authored (80/label, split 40/20/20 per label).  The
caller-owned routing guide and labeling policy are unchanged.

Because the v1 rows are preserved, their v1 raw decisions remain valid.  The v2
capture reused those 160 records and made 320 new live decisions against the same
pinned Qwen3-1.7B (seed 7).  The v2 ledger/records pair is the v1 pair plus the new
decisions; `raw-v2.jsonl` is the fit input.

The new rows are deliberately harder than v1, so accuracy is not comparable
across corpus revisions:

| subset | n | accuracy |
|---|---:|---:|
| v1 rows | 160 | 0.7750 |
| v2 new rows | 320 | 0.6813 |
| v2 test, v1 rows | 40 | 0.8000 |
| v2 test, new rows | 80 | 0.6625 |

## Fit and test metrics

The procedure is unchanged: temperature scaling and isotonic are fitted on
train, selected on validation ECE (Brier tie-break), refit on train+validation,
then evaluated once on test.  The test split is not used for selection.
Validation ECE selected isotonic (`0.049737`) over temperature (`0.069338`).

| metric (test n=120) | raw entropy | raw top | calibrated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ECE | 0.210984 | 0.248294 | **0.026684** |
| Brier | 0.220948 | 0.238044 | **0.172292** |
| accuracy | — | 0.708333 | 0.708333 |

Deltas vs raw top probability:

- ΔECE **-0.221610**, 95% CI **[-0.262469, -0.071803]**
- ΔBrier **-0.065752**, 95% CI **[-0.114074, -0.017392]**

Both intervals exclude zero at this n.  The interval is a paired bootstrap over
these 120 examples with seed 42 and 2,000 resamples; it is not a cross-domain or
cross-machine interval.

The calibrated reliability bins on test are close to the diagonal (for example,
24 examples in `[0.9,1.0]` have mean confidence 0.9137 and accuracy 0.9167; 40
examples in `[0.6,0.7)` have 0.6447 and 0.6750).  The artifact also records
fit-in-sample metrics; they are not evidence of generalization.

## M3 interaction

The separate model-level replay audit (`harness/replay_audit.py`) re-decided eight
v2 test examples after the capture completed.  All eight matched on `ref`, `key`,
`model_pin`, raw `probabilities`, and `contentHash` under quiescent load
(`identity_ok=true`, `distribution_ok=true`, strict `ok=true`).

During the concurrent capture run, strict replay of v1 `ex-001` preserved
`ref`/`key`/`model_pin` but produced small probability differences and a different
`contentHash`.  The quiescent retest matched, so this is recorded as a
concurrency/load sensitivity observation, not a calibration or decoding result.
See `RR-M3-20261023.md`.

## Honest limitations

- 480 support intents authored by this project, not a production sample.  The new
  rows are harder; the accuracy drop is expected and is not evidence about the
  calibration mapping.
- Per pin, label set, seed, machine, and runner build.  Do not transfer this
  artifact to another model or domain without re-fitting.
- The artifact estimates P(key correct) on this input distribution; it is not a
  correctness guarantee.  Raw probabilities, `ref`, and `contentHash` are
  unchanged; calibration is an optional sidecar.
- The v1 n=40 caveat remains.  v2's n=120 intervals exclude zero for these
  deltas, but the sample is still single-corpus and single-machine.
- ODPS/ODCS packaging, hosted execution, and the hub leaderboard remain deferred
  (T14: local-only now, hybrid later).
