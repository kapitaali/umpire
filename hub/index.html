umpire Calibration Corpus

umpire-calcorpus-0.1

The umpire M2 calibration corpus v2 drop: 480 project-authored support-intent examples (4 labels, 120/label, split 240/120/120), the caller-owned routing guide inside every state, 480 live decisions captured from a pinned, hash-stamped Qwen3-1.7B local GGUF (seed 7) under a JSON-schema constrained grammar, a 481-event xCABI-0.1 decision ledger, 480 payload records, an isotonic calibration artifact (cal-v1:aceb881587b3b3a6), a calibrated sidecar on the 120 untouched test rows, and the deterministic page-table replay baseline. On the test split, isotonic calibration reduces ECE 0.2483 (raw top) → 0.0267 and Brier 0.2380 → 0.1723 with accuracy unchanged (0.7083); paired-bootstrap 95% CIs exclude zero. Honest limits: single project-authored corpus, one pin/seed/machine/runner build, n=120 directional vs the retained v1 n=40 caveat; raw probabilities, ref and contentHash are unchanged by calibration; accuracy is not comparable across corpus revisions (v2 rows are deliberately harder); the artifact estimates P(key correct) — it is not a correctness guarantee.
